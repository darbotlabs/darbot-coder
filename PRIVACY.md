# Privacy Policy for darbot-coder

## Research Project Notice

darbot-coder is an open-source research project developed by Darbot Labs. This tool:

- Operates entirely locally within your VSCode environment
- Does not collect or transmit personal data to Darbot Labs
- API keys and configurations remain on your local machine
- No telemetry data is sent to Darbot Labs servers

## Data Processing

- All code processing happens locally on your machine
- API calls go directly to your configured AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.)
- No intermediary servers or data collection by Darbot Labs
- Your source code never leaves your local environment unless you explicitly send it to AI providers

## Third-Party Services

When you configure AI providers (like OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.), their respective privacy policies apply to any data you send to them through darbot-coder.

## Open Source Commitment

This project is transparent and open-source. Review our complete code at:
https://github.com/darbotlabs/darbot-coder

## Research Purpose

This tool is provided for research and development purposes. We make no warranties about its suitability for production use.
